What we do

Three words. One working method.

Babasaheb asked the oppressed to educate themselves, agitate for their rights, and organize for collective strength. We've built our entire program model around that same sequence — reframed for rural children today as Education, Health & Wellbeing, and Career & Livelihood.

Why we exist

Access shouldn't depend on your postal code.

Millions of children in rural and under-privileged communities grow up within reach of a school, a clinic and a job market they can never quite touch — blocked by distance, cost, or simply never having anyone show them the way in. Neo Nitin Foundation was founded to close that gap, one village at a time.

We work directly with local communities, government schools and panchayats to design programs that fit the village, not the other way round.

  • 100% free — no tuition, consultation or program fees for families
  • Community-led — local volunteers and para-workers run every centre
  • Transparent — audited annually, reports published every year
