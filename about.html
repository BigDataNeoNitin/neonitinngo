Our story

Twelve years of walking into villages others walk past.

Neo Nitin Foundation began with one volunteer, one classroom and a belief Babasaheb Ambedkar spent his life proving true: that education is the first act of self-respect.

Since 2011
How it started

A single evening class in one village

Neo Nitin Foundation was founded in 2011 by a small group of teachers and public health workers who noticed the same pattern across the villages they served: children capable and curious, held back only by distance from a school, a clinic, or anyone who believed their future was worth investing in.

What started as a single evening learning class has grown into a network of education centres, mobile health camps and career training programs spread across six states — built one relationship, one panchayat, one classroom at a time.

We remain guided by the same three-word method Dr. B.R. Ambedkar gave to India's most marginalized communities: Educate. Agitate. Organize. For us, that translates into free schooling, active advocacy for children's rights, and organizing communities around shared resources like health camps and skill centres.

Mission & vision

What we're working toward

Mission

Remove the barriers between rural children and their right to learn, stay healthy, and earn a dignified livelihood.

Vision

A generation of rural India that reads, questions, and organizes for itself — needing no one to speak on its behalf.

Guiding philosophy

"Educate, Agitate, Organize"

Dr. Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar — Babasaheb — gave India's oppressed communities a three-step method for their own liberation: learn first, so you understand your condition; agitate, so your voice is heard; organize, so that voice cannot be ignored.

Every program we run maps back to one of these three words. Our learning centres are the "Educate." Our advocacy with panchayats and schools for enrolment and children's rights is the "Agitate." Our community health camps and skill cooperatives are the "Organize" — villages pooling resources so no single family carries the weight alone.

Our journey

From one classroom to six states

2011

First learning centre opens

A single evening class begins in one village in Uttar Pradesh, run by four volunteer teachers.

2014

Health camps launch

Partnering with local doctors, our first mobile health camp reaches 300 families in a single weekend.

2017

Registered as a public trust

Neo Nitin Foundation formally registers, achieving 12A and 80G certification for transparent, tax-deductible giving.

2020

Career & skilling program begins

Vocational training centres open for youth and women, with direct placement partnerships with local businesses.

2026

186 villages, 6 states

Today our programs reach over 12,000 children and their families across six states, with 340+ health camps held to date.
