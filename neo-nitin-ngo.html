Who we are

A ground-level NGO, organized around Babasaheb's philosophy.

Neo Nitin NGO was formed to carry Babasaheb's three-word instruction into daily, practical work — not as a slogan, but as a method. We believe education breaks the first chain, awareness (agitation of conscience, not conflict) breaks the second, and organization is what makes the change permanent.

We work directly with first-generation learners, women's collectives, and daily-wage families across our partner districts — running learning centres, rights-awareness camps, and self-help groups that are built to outlast any single donation cycle.

Every rupee, every volunteer hour, and every partnership is measured against one question: does this make someone more educated, more aware, or more organized?

Our method

Not a tagline. A working method.

Babasaheb's instruction gives us three departments of work — each feeding the next.

01 — Educate

Learning centres & scholarships

We run after-school learning centres, digital-literacy labs, and scholarship pipelines for children who would otherwise be the first in their family to finish school.

  • 120+ children in after-school centres
  • Digital literacy labs in 6 villages
  • Merit & need-based scholarships
02 — Agitate

Rights awareness & legal aid

"Agitate" for us means raising conscience, not conflict — camps that teach constitutional rights, legal-aid clinics, and campaigns against discrimination and exclusion.

  • Constitutional-rights awareness camps
  • Free legal-aid helpdesk
  • Anti-discrimination campaigns
03 — Organize

Self-help groups & community councils

Change that isn't organized doesn't last. We help form women's self-help groups, youth committees, and village welfare councils that keep running long after we step back.

  • Women's self-help & savings groups
  • Youth & student committees
  • Village-level welfare councils
