Our programs

Three programs, one method: educate, empower, organize.

Every program is 100% free to the families we serve and run in partnership with local schools, health workers and panchayats.

01 · Education

Free Learning Centres & Scholarships

Our education program keeps first-generation learners enrolled, on grade level, and moving toward secondary school and beyond. Each village centre runs daily after-school sessions in reading, math and basic digital literacy, staffed by trained community volunteers.

Where families face the added barrier of school fees, uniforms or transport, we provide direct scholarships so cost is never the reason a child drops out.

02 · Health & Welfare

Mobile Health Camps & Nutrition Support

For families in villages with no nearby clinic, our mobile health camps bring doctors, basic diagnostics and medicine directly to the community — free of charge, every month.

Camps focus on maternal and child health, nutrition screening, vaccination drives, and early detection of common conditions, with referrals to partner hospitals for anything requiring specialist care.

03 · Career & Livelihood

Vocational Training & Placement

For youth and women who are ready to earn, we run certified vocational training in trades like tailoring, electrical work, computer basics and retail — followed by direct placement support with local and partner businesses.

The goal isn't just a certificate. It's a first paycheck, and the confidence that comes with it.
